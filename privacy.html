Privacy

Short and simple.

Your files stay on your device

Every tool on this website runs inside your own browser. When you choose a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or picture file, it is read and changed on your computer or phone. It is never uploaded to our servers, because the website has no server that receives files.

What we collect

We do not ask you to sign up, and we do not collect your name, email or files. This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Libraries from other websites

To work, the page loads open-source code libraries (for reading and writing PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files) from public content delivery networks such as cdnjs and jsDelivr. Those services can see that your browser asked for the library, like with any website, but they never see your files.

Hosting

The website itself is hosted by a normal web host, which may keep standard server logs (such as the time of a visit and your IP address). We do not use these logs to look at your files, because we never receive them.

Questions

If you have a question about this page, contact the site owner through the details on the home page of the owner's website.
