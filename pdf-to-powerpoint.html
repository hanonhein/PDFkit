PDF to PowerPoint

    About PDF to PowerPoint

    Make a PowerPoint file (.pptx) with one slide for each page of your PDF. The slides look like the pages but are pictures.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your PDF.
    2. Click the button.
    3. Download the .pptx file and open it in PowerPoint or Google Slides.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • One slide per page.
    • The slides look just like your pages.
    • Your PDF is never uploaded.

    Questions

    Can I edit the text on the slides?

    The slides are pictures of your pages, so the text is not editable. This keeps the look exactly the same.

    How do I turn a PDF into slides?

    Choose the PDF and click the button. You download a .pptx file with one slide per page.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
