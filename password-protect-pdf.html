Password protect PDF

    About Password protect PDF

    Lock a PDF with a password. Only people who know the password can open it. It uses strong AES-256 encryption.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your PDF.
    2. Type a password twice.
    3. Click the button and download the locked PDF.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • AES-256 encryption.
    • The password stays on your device.
    • No account.

    Questions

    How do I password protect a PDF?

    Choose the PDF, type your password twice and click the button. The new file asks for the password when opened.

    Can you recover my password?

    No. It never leaves your device, so there is nothing we can recover. Keep it somewhere safe.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
