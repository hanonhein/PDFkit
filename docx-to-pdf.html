DOCX to PDF

    About DOCX to PDF

    Turn a .docx file into a PDF that looks the same on every device. The conversion runs in your browser, so your document is not uploaded.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your .docx file.
    2. Click the button.
    3. Download the PDF.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • Share a document that cannot be edited by accident.
    • No Word needed.
    • Private, since nothing is uploaded.

    Questions

    How do I convert DOCX to PDF?

    Choose the .docx file here and click the button. The PDF downloads to your device.

    Does it work with files from Google Docs?

    Yes. In Google Docs choose File, Download, Microsoft Word (.docx), then convert that file here.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
