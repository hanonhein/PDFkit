Edit PDF

    About Edit PDF

    Draw, highlight, sign and add text to a PDF. Everything happens in your browser, and your file stays on your device.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your PDF.
    2. Pick a tool: pen, highlight, text or eraser, and a colour.
    3. Click Save PDF and download your edited file.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • Pen, highlighter, text, eraser and undo.
    • Move or delete anything you added.
    • Your PDF is never uploaded.

    Questions

    How do I add text to a PDF?

    Open the PDF here, choose the text tool, click on the page and type. Then save the PDF.

    Can I change the original words in a PDF?

    This tool adds drawings, highlights and text on top of the page. It does not rewrite the original text.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
