PDF to Excel

    About PDF to Excel

    Make an Excel file (.xlsx) from your PDF. Each page becomes a sheet and each line a row. Tables work best.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your PDF.
    2. Click the button.
    3. Download the Excel file and open it in Excel or Google Sheets.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • Each page becomes a sheet.
    • Works for Google Sheets too.
    • Your PDF is never uploaded.

    Questions

    How do I get a table from a PDF into Excel?

    Convert the PDF here and open the .xlsx file. PDFs with clear tables give the best result.

    Why are some columns not perfect?

    A PDF does not store tables as tables, so columns are guessed from the gaps between words. You may need to tidy a few cells.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
