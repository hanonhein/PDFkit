Remove password

    About Remove password

    Take the password off a PDF when you know it. Only unlock files you are allowed to open.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your locked PDF.
    2. Type the password.
    3. Click the button and download the PDF without a password.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • Opens with the password you know.
    • Works on your device.
    • Your PDF is never uploaded.

    Questions

    How do I remove the password from a PDF?

    Choose the PDF, type its password and click the button. You get a copy that opens without a password.

    Can you open a PDF if I do not know the password?

    No. This tool only works with the correct password. It does not guess or crack passwords.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
