Excel to PDF

    About Excel to PDF

    Turn an Excel file (.xlsx, .xls or .csv) into a PDF. Every sheet becomes a table. For Google Sheets, download the file as .xlsx first.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your Excel or CSV file.
    2. Click the button.
    3. Download the PDF.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • Works with .xlsx, .xls and .csv.
    • Every sheet is included.
    • Your spreadsheet is never uploaded.

    Questions

    How do I save an Excel sheet as a PDF?

    Choose your file here and click the button. Each sheet becomes a table in the PDF.

    Can I convert a CSV file to PDF?

    Yes. CSV files are accepted as well as Excel files.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
