Organize pages

    About Organize pages

    See every page of your PDF as a picture. Rotate pages, delete the ones you do not need and change their order.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your PDF. Every page appears as a small picture.
    2. Use the buttons under each page to rotate, delete or move it.
    3. Save the new PDF.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • See what you are changing.
    • Rotate, delete and reorder in one place.
    • Everything happens on your device.

    Questions

    How do I rotate one page in a PDF?

    Open the PDF here and click the rotate button under that page. Then save the new file.

    How do I delete pages from a PDF?

    Click the delete button under each page you do not want, then save. Your original file is not changed.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
