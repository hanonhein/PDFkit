Protect PDF

    About Protect PDF

    Add a password so only people who know it can open the PDF. It is locked with strong AES-256 encryption.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your PDF.
    2. Type a password twice.
    3. Click the button and download your locked PDF.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • Strong AES-256 encryption.
    • The password never leaves your device.
    • No account needed.

    Questions

    How do I put a password on a PDF?

    Choose the PDF, type a password twice and click the button. The new PDF asks for the password when it is opened.

    What if I forget the password?

    We cannot recover it, because it never leaves your device. Write it down somewhere safe.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
