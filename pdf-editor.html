PDF editor

    About PDF editor

    Edit a PDF in your browser. Draw, highlight, add text, erase and undo. When you are done, save the new PDF to your device.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your PDF.
    2. Pick a tool and a colour, then mark up the page.
    3. Click Save PDF and download the result.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • Pen, highlighter, text and eraser.
    • Move or delete anything you added.
    • Your PDF is never uploaded.

    Questions

    Is this PDF editor really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no time limit.

    Can I change the original text inside a PDF?

    No. You can add text, drawings and highlights on top of the page. The original text is not rewritten. To get editable text, convert the PDF to Word first.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
