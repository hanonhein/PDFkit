Sign PDF

    About Sign PDF

    Draw your signature and place it on the page. Your file and your signature stay on your device.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your PDF.
    2. Draw your signature with your mouse or finger.
    3. Place it on the page, resize it and save the PDF.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • Draw with mouse, finger or pen.
    • Move and resize the signature.
    • Your signature is never saved on a server.

    Questions

    How do I sign a PDF without printing it?

    Open the PDF here, draw your signature, put it where you need it and save the new PDF.

    Is my signature stored anywhere?

    No. It is kept only in your browser while the page is open and is gone when you close it.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
