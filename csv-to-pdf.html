CSV to PDF

    About CSV to PDF

    Turn a CSV file into a PDF table. Excel files (.xlsx and .xls) work too, and every sheet becomes a table.

    How to use it

    1. Choose your CSV or Excel file.
    2. Click the button.
    3. Download the PDF.

    Why use PDF Editor Kit

    • CSV, XLSX and XLS are accepted.
    • Neat tables you can print.
    • Your data never leaves your device.

    Questions

    How do I print a CSV file as a PDF?

    Choose the CSV file here and click the button. You get a PDF table you can print or send.

    Is my data uploaded?

    No. The file is read in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on how many files you use and no watermark added to your result.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool runs inside your browser, so your file never leaves your device. You can read the details on the privacy page.
